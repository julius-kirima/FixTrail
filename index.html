ABOUT FIXTRAIL

Making Facility Maintenance Easier

FixTrail is a shared-facility fault reporting and tracking system that helps students report problems with computers, internet connections, projectors, and other equipment.

Administrators can review submitted reports, monitor repair progress, and update fault statuses.
